# Steno

Personal macOS app that records meetings, produces a Transcript and a Summary with providers chosen by the user (local or hosted in the EU) and writes the result into an Obsidian Vault. An alternative to Granola that complies with the company policy on EU data residency.

## Language

### Meeting and capture

**Meeting** (`Meeting`):
A call recorded with Steno, from start to stop. Everything else revolves around it.
_Avoid_: call, session

**Recording** (`Recording`):
The audio of a Meeting, kept outside the Vault for a limited time.
_Avoid_: audio, audio file

**Track** (`Track`):
One of the two audio sources of a Recording: **Me** (microphone) or **Others** (system audio).
_Avoid_: channel, stream

**Segment** (`Segment`):
A portion of a Track saved in its own file, with its start time relative to the start of the Meeting.
_Avoid_: chunk, block, piece

**Capture mode** (`CaptureMode`):
How a Meeting is captured: **Call** (separate Me and Others Tracks) or **Room** (microphone only, for in-person meetings, without Me/Others attribution). Only Call exists in v1.

**Processing** (`Processing`):
The phase after the stop in which Steno completes the Transcript and generates the Summary. It can fail and be retried.
_Avoid_: post-processing, job

### Outputs

**Transcript** (`Transcript`):
The spoken text of a Meeting, with timestamps and who speaks: Me or a Speaker. It lives in its own file in the Vault.
_Avoid_: transcription (for the file)

**Utterance** (`Utterance`):
A stretch of speech recognised in a Track, with start and end relative to the start of the Meeting. Consecutive Utterances of the same Track and Speaker form a paragraph of the Transcript.
_Avoid_: segment (that is something else), sentence, chunk

**Speaker** (`Utterance.speaker`):
One of the voices told apart in the Others Track, shown as *Speaker 1*, *Speaker 2*… in the order they first speak. The same number is the same voice within one Meeting only; it says nothing about who the person is. An Others Utterance that cannot be given a Speaker stays *Others*.
_Avoid_: participant (the people in the Meeting, Me included), voice

**Summary** (`Summary`):
The structured summary of a Meeting, generated by applying a Template to the Transcript and the Personal notes.
_Avoid_: recap, minutes

**Personal notes** (`PersonalNotes`):
The notes the user writes by hand during the Meeting. They guide the Summary and Steno never changes them.
_Avoid_: user notes

**Meeting note** (`MeetingNote`):
The file in the Vault holding the Summary, the Personal notes and the link to the Transcript.
_Avoid_: summary file, document

**Managed section** (`ManagedSection`):
The part of the Meeting note that Steno may rewrite. Everything else belongs to the user.

**Template** (`Template`):
A file in the Vault that defines the structure and instructions of the Summary for a kind of Meeting.
_Avoid_: model (ambiguous with the AI model), prompt

**Vocabulary** (`Vocabulary`):
A file in the Vault listing the terms (names, acronyms, technical words) that speech recognition tends to get wrong. Each entry is the correct term, optionally with the variants usually heard instead and a short description of what it is. It applies to every Meeting.
_Avoid_: dictionary, glossary

**Regeneration** (`Regeneration`):
Producing a new Summary for a Meeting already processed from its Transcript in the Vault, without the audio. It is what Retry does once the audio is deleted.

### Providers

**Role** (`Role`):
What a Provider is used for: **transcription** or **summary**. In v1 only the summary Role is configurable; transcription is always local.

**Provider** (`Provider`):
A local or remote service that performs a Role. It must process data inside the EU.
_Avoid_: backend, API, vendor

**Provider profile** (`ProviderProfile`):
A named configuration of a Provider (endpoint, credentials, model) that the user selects for a Role. For the Summary the user can also choose none (*Transcript* in the menu): the Meeting then gets only its Transcript, with no Summary and no Template.
_Avoid_: preset, account

**Vault** (`Vault`):
The Obsidian folder where Steno writes Meeting notes and Transcripts and reads Templates from.
